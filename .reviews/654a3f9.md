# Review of `654a3f9`

## Finding: failed and unknown outcomes are not represented in the modeled log

**Severity:** medium  
**Files:** `spec/KeyInventory.tla`, `spec/README.md`

The README says the model covers plan entries logged before execution and signed results afterwards. In the model, however, `Submit` and `Confirm` only change the `plans` variable; neither records a plan event in `log`. `ExecuteFailed` and `ExecuteUnknown` also leave `log` unchanged. As a result, the model cannot check that those outcomes are durably recorded, or that a later recovery can distinguish a failed plan from an operation whose result was lost.

Extend the event history to include plan, confirmation, and every result (`succeeded`, `failed`, `unknown`), and check that each executed plan has the matching prior authorization and exactly one terminal result. Alternatively, explicitly document that plan and result logging is abstracted away and outside the properties TLC checks.

The inventory safety properties may still hold under the current abstraction, but the stated scope of the model is broader than the behavior it represents.
